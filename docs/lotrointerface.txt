[SIZE="4"][B]GrommeyUI[/B][/SIZE]
A clean, modern all-in-one interface for The Lord of the Rings Online: flat, opaque frames, one accent colour, and everything can be set and moved where you want.

[B]Features[/B]
[LIST]
[*][B]Bags[/B]: all your bags in one window, sorted by category, with your own categories (hold Shift while dragging an item to drop it into any category), search, new items, money and currencies.
[*][B]Unit frames[/B]: player, target, target of target and party frames replacing the game ones, with buffs and debuffs, the class resource of every class and power bars you can place anywhere. Click a frame to target.
[*][B]Info bar[/B]: money, currencies, bag slots, durability, FPS, time, session time and money, character.
[*][B]Auras[/B]: your buffs and debuffs in two areas next to the minimap, with detailed tooltips, important and hidden effects.
[*][B]Timer bars[/B]: your buffs and debuffs as bars that empty as they run out, with their own filters.
[*][B]Buff reminders[/B]: a warning when a buff you chose is missing or about to end.
[*][B]Action bars[/B]: extra bars for skills, items and commands, a consumables bar that fills itself from your backpack and a travel bar with your travel skills and mounts.
[*][B]Combat meter[/B]: replaces Combat Analysis for your character. Damage done and taken, enemies, healing and power, fight by fight: key figures, bars by skill or target, a summary and a detail window with every counter. Fights are kept between reloads.
[*][B]Themes[/B]: six accent colours, three backgrounds, three fonts and a text size.
[*][B]Profiles[/B]: shared between characters, exported and imported as a text code.
[*][B]Move mode[/B]: drag every frame where you want, with a grid and snapping.
[*][B]Setup assistant[/B] on first launch, and a What's new window after each update.
[*]In [B]English, French and German[/B].
[/LIST]

[B]Installation[/B]
Unzip the file into [I]Documents\The Lord of the Rings Online\Plugins\[/I]: it holds the [I]GrommeyUI[/I] folder, nothing to rename. In game, load [B]GrommeyUI[/B] from the plugin manager or type [I]/plugins load GrommeyUI[/I]. Do not load [I]~GrommeyUIReloader[/I] yourself: GrommeyUI uses it to reload itself.

[B]Commands[/B]
[LIST]
[*][I]/gui[/I]: open or close the options
[*][I]/gui move[/I]: move the frames
[*][I]/gui reload[/I]: reload the interface
[*][I]/gui reset[/I]: put every frame back in place
[*][I]/gui meter[/I]: show or hide the combat meter
[/LIST]

Source code and bug reports: [URL="https://github.com/JeromeM/GrommeyUI"]github.com/JeromeM/GrommeyUI[/URL]

[SIZE="3"][B]Français[/B][/SIZE]
Une interface moderne et épurée pour Le Seigneur des Anneaux Online : fenêtres plates et opaques, une couleur de thème, et tout se règle et se place où on veut. Sacs regroupés et triés par catégorie (avec tes propres catégories), cadres joueur / cible / cible de la cible / groupe, barre d'infos, auras près de la minimap, barres de temps des buffs et débuffs, rappels de buffs, barres d'action avec une barre de consommables automatique et une barre Voyage, compteur de combat pour remplacer Combat Analysis, thèmes, profils partageables par code, mode déplacement, assistant de configuration. [*]Unit frames: the class resource of every class (fervour, focus, attunement...), power and resource bars you can place anywhere, and bars that glide to their new value.
[*]Action bars: a Travel bar with the travel skills and mounts of your character, and your own order for the consumables bar.
[*]Bags: hold Shift while dragging an item to drop it into any category. Your empty categories only show up then.
[*]Combat meter: the damage of your pets, added to yours or shown apart.
[*]Options: a bigger window, a menu in categories you can fold, and clearer help notes. Areas keep their place when they change size.
